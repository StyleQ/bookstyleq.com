## What StyleQ is

A **fully managed, on-site salon and spa service** for senior living communities. Licensed, elder-care-trained stylists come to the community on a recurring schedule and handle everything — scheduling, supplies, and family billing.

## Who we're selling to (the reader of our copy)

The **Campus Administrator / Executive Director** of a senior living community (200+ residents, with Assisted Living and Memory Care). They are stretched thin, burned by monopoly incumbent vendors, and afraid of three things: **more work, staff pushback, and a regulatory complaint.** Their gut-check on any vendor: _"Does this create work or solve problems?"_

## Core positioning (the most important rule)

**We are a reliability and peace-of-mind partner, NOT a "beauty brand."** Lead with calm operations, consistency, dignity, and survey readiness. Beauty is the *how*; outsourced peace of mind is the *what we sell*.

## The value proposition

> A zero-hassle resident amenity that lifts family satisfaction — without adding anything to your staff's workload, your budget, or your survey risk.

## Objections we must answer proactively

| Fear | The answer to surface early |
|---|---|
| **Cost** | The facility does **not** pay. We bill residents/families directly. Say this plainly and early. |
| **Compliance** | We are survey-ready: licensed, insured, infection-control conscious, consistent. |
| **Staff burden** | **Zero lift.** We handle scheduling, reminders, coordination, and billing. Staff do nothing. |
| **Risk of a bad vendor** | Consistent, vetted stylists + a low-risk pilot before any commitment. |

## Voice & word choice

**Lean into:** peace of mind, handled, zero-lift, dignity, consistency, licensed & insured, survey-ready, residents look forward to it, families notice, calm.

**Avoid:** language that frames us as a luxury/beauty indulgence, anything implying the facility pays or takes on work, and heavy "sign a contract" pressure.

**Tone:** warm and human toward residents and families; reassuring and operationally credible toward administrators. Both at once — this is a trust sale in a word-of-mouth market.

## Calls to action

Favor the **Trojan Horse / phased pilot** over a contract ask:
- Primary: "Book a Consultation" / "Request a Risk-Free Pilot"
- Foot-in-the-door for smaller/loyal accounts: "Add Supplemental Services" (e.g. pedicures) alongside an existing hairdresser.

## Proof points to weave in

Licensed & fully insured · trained for elder care (memory care, limited mobility, sensitive skin) · we bring everything (chairs, warm towels, products) · zero setup · we handle scheduling, reminders, and family billing.

## SEO note

Primary intent is B2B ("on-site salon for senior living," "mobile stylist for assisted living / memory care," "senior living spa services"), NOT consumer beauty terms. Keep meta, headings, and structured data aligned to the administrator's search intent, not a walk-in salon's.
